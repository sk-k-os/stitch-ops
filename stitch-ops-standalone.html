Process

Four steps from idea to delivered.

01 / Describe

Tell us what you want

Describe the garment, upload a photo, or let our AI find the exact SKU. Specify logo placement: chest, sleeve, back, or custom.

02 / Preview

See it before we stitch

Our mockup engine places your logo on the garment in real time. Approve the design, request a revision, or swap the blank.

03 / Confirm

One checkbox, zero friction

Verify the mockup, confirm quantities, and check out. We handle pickup, embroidery, quality control, and delivery.

04 / Receive

24-hour turnaround

Finished pieces delivered to your office or event venue. Same-day available for Enterprise tier clients.
